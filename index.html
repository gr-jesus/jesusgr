Candidato a Investigador Nacional (SNII) 2024-2027

Jesús García Ramírez currently serves as an interim professor at the Unidad Profesional Interdisciplinaria de Ingeniería Campus Tlaxcala. Previously, he was an adjunct professor at the Universidad Politécnica Metropolitana de Hidalgo, where he has supervised four Master's theses in Artificial Intelligence. He has also completed two postdoctoral fellowships, one at the Faculty of Engineering at UNAM and another at the UAEMex Center in Zumpango. In 2024, he was admitted as a Candidate Researcher in the National System of Researchers (SNII). He holds a B.S. in Computer Systems Engineering from the Instituto Tecnológico de Pachuca, an M.S. in Computer Science from the Benemérita Universidad Autónoma de Puebla, and a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Instituto Nacional de Astrofísica, Óptica y Electrónica.
